1. What this policy covers

This policy describes how the current Bibi iPhone app handles information in Shorts, Circles, Challenges, Chats and Me. "We" means the operator of Bibi identified above. It distinguishes information processed on your device from information handled by external services.

Bibi currently has no account, messaging, content-upload or moderation backend. Your profile, imported content, social activity and chat outbox are stored locally. Accepting the agreements starts a local session; it does not create or authenticate an online account. Bibi does not currently synchronize these records between devices.

Local storage does not mean the app never connects to the internet. Apple services handle available in-app purchases, and opening an online agreement contacts Supabase. External websites and any device backups you enable have separate data practices, explained below.

2. Information processed on your device

2.1 Profile and agreement acceptance

Bibi stores the display name, biography and avatar you choose so that your profile can appear in the app. It records the agreement version and acceptance time. The current entry flow does not request a telephone number, email address, password, government ID or date of birth, and does not use Sign in with Apple or another identity provider.

2.2 Videos, photos and creation records

When you select media using the iOS picker, Bibi receives the selected item and saves an app-owned copy or a processed image. Video records include a local identifier, title, duration, import time and saved status. Bibi generates a cover image from the selected video. Submission records include your title, caption, category, creation time and local status. The creation flow also asks you to confirm that you have permission to use the selected content.

Imported videos may contain sound and embedded metadata from the selected file. Bibi does not promise to remove all embedded metadata. It does not separately extract location coordinates for a location feature. Circle photos are resized and encoded for local display; profile images and video covers are also stored locally.

Bibi does not upload these files to a Bibi server. A pending submission is a local record, not proof of public posting, delivery or review. Removing an app-owned copy does not delete the original item in Photos.

2.3 Shorts activity and viewing history

Bibi stores likes, saved-video choices, comments, replies and local share-action counts. It records video identifiers and their latest viewing times to order recommendations on this device. Viewing history is limited to the 2,000 most recent video records. These records are not sent to an analytics service by Bibi.

2.4 Circles and Challenges

Bibi stores the circles you join, authored posts and replies, selected photos, attached-video references, saved posts and creation times. A post or reply may retain the author name and avatar reference used when it was created, even if you later edit your profile.

Challenge records link a challenge to a selected local video and a creation time. Withdrawing or replacing a challenge entry does not itself delete the underlying video; manage that video separately. These records remain on your device in the current version.

2.5 Chats and relationships

Bibi stores drafts, messages placed in the local outbox, conversation identifiers, author labels, text and timestamps. It also stores local groups and preferences such as pinning, read or unread marks, muting, hiding, and follow or relationship choices.

There is no live message-delivery service. An outbox item or a local read mark does not mean another person received or read anything. Built-in fictional profiles and authored conversations are supplied app content, not evidence that those people are online. Bibi does not send your messages to an AI service.

2.6 Reports and blocks

A report can store the content identifier and title, author identifier and name, selected reason, details you enter and report time. Blocking stores the relevant profile reference. Bibi uses this information to hide content and apply local interaction restrictions. The current report action does not send a report to staff or an external moderation provider.

2.7 Diamonds and purchase records

The local wallet stores your diamond balance, credit and spending activity, dates and credited transaction identifiers. A Circle gift record can include the selected content and creator identifiers, content title, gift type, quantity and diamond cost. These records support balance display and help prevent processing the same purchase callback twice.

Apple supplies product and transaction information through StoreKit when its purchasing service is available. Bibi does not receive your full payment-card number, card security code, billing address or Apple Account password through its purchase flow. Bibi has no separate payment processor or server-side wallet in the current version.

3. Camera, media selection and permissions

Camera access is optional and requested when you open a camera feature. Bibi uses camera frames for your own preview on the call screen and to scan a friend QR code. The preview is not a live connection to another person. Camera frames are not recorded or transmitted by Bibi. A scanned code is checked against local profile references; the raw camera feed is not stored.

The current version does not capture microphone audio or provide live voice or video communication. Audio already included in a video you select can be copied and played with that video.

The iOS photo picker gives Bibi access to the items you choose; the app does not request unrestricted access to browse your entire photo library. If an item is stored in iCloud, iOS may retrieve it to complete your selection under your Apple settings.

You can deny camera access and continue using features that do not need it. Change access in iOS Settings under Bibi or Privacy & Security. Revoking camera access does not remove media you previously selected; use the app's deletion controls for those copies.

Bibi does not request access to your contacts, precise location, health data or advertising tracking permission. It has no advertising, third-party analytics or third-party crash-reporting SDK integrated into the current app.

4. Information handled outside your device

4.1 Apple

StoreKit can contact Apple to obtain product information and process payments, including when the wallet initializes. Apple handles its account, payment, purchase, security and network information under its own terms and privacy notices. Apple may also process operating-system diagnostics according to your device settings. This is separate from Bibi's local activity records.

Apple's App Store privacy notice: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/app-store/

4.2 Supabase-hosted agreements

Opening Terms of Service or Privacy Policy in the app attempts to load a PDF from Supabase Storage. Supabase and its infrastructure providers can receive ordinary web-request information, including your IP address, browser or device information, requested file and request time. Bibi does not append your local profile, messages, media or wallet records to that request. The agreement PDFs are publicly accessible without signing in.

The agreement reader uses a nonpersistent web data store rather than a lasting in-app cookie store. This does not prevent Supabase or its infrastructure providers from receiving or retaining server-side request data. If online loading fails, Bibi displays a bundled saved copy. The initial online request may already have occurred before that fallback.

Supabase's privacy policy: https://supabase.com/privacy

4.3 External links, backups and support

Opening an external credits, licensing or other website subjects that visit to the site's and browser's privacy practices. An in-app local sharing action is not, by itself, delivery to an external recipient.

Bibi does not run a cloud-backup service. iOS or computer backups may include app data depending on your settings. Deleting data inside Bibi does not erase previously created backups; manage those through Apple or the backup provider.

If you email trvahai996@icloud.com, the operator and its email provider will receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. Do not include passwords, full payment-card details or unnecessary private media. The designated support address uses iCloud Mail. A specific support storage location and support-retention period have not yet been designated.

5. Purposes and sharing limits

Bibi processes local information to display and edit your content, remember preferences, order local recommendations, apply local safety controls, record agreement acceptance and maintain the wallet. Optional camera and media actions are initiated by you.

Bibi does not currently sell personal information, share it for targeted advertising, or track you across other companies' apps and websites. It does not transmit your local profile, posts, chat outbox or reports to Bibi-hosted servers, advertisers, data brokers or AI providers.

If the operator receives information directly through support, it should be used to address the request, resolve related disputes and meet applicable legal obligations. Any legally required disclosure must be limited to information actually held and the relevant request. This does not give the operator remote access to your local library.

This policy is a notice of data practices, not blanket consent to unrelated uses. Where consent or another legal basis is required for processing outside your device, it must be established for the specific purpose. A future backend, analytics service or new recipient must be disclosed before the new processing begins.

6. Storage, retention and security

Bibi keeps its application records and imported media in its iOS app container. Core records use atomic writes and iOS file protection until the first device unlock after a restart. Imported video copies also use iOS file protection. These measures do not guarantee security against an unlocked, compromised or shared device.

Local profile, content, chat, safety, agreement and wallet records generally remain until you remove them through an available control, delete the local account, or delete the app and its data. There is no automatic fixed-day deletion schedule for all records. Viewing history has the record limit described above. Older local records retained for compatibility are removed with the local account.

Bibi has no remote library from which it can recover erased media, finished consumable purchases or a deleted wallet. Apple or Supabase records and device backups follow their own retention controls. Offloading an app can preserve its data and is not the same as deleting it.

7. Your controls and deletion

You can edit your name, biography and avatar in Me > Settings > Edit profile. Use the available content controls to remove your own posts, replies, imported videos or pending submissions, and to change saved or relationship choices. Settings provides Blocked and Reported lists for managing local safety choices.

To erase Bibi's local account data, open Me > Settings > Delete account and confirm. This removes the local profile, app-owned imported media, community and challenge records, chat state and outbox, viewing and interaction records, reports, blocks, agreement acceptance and wallet data, including the local balance and transaction history. The app then returns to an empty local state.

Deleting an individual post or video does not necessarily remove every associated record, such as a separate report, viewing-history entry or wallet gift record. Use Delete account for removal of the full local data set. If deletion reports an error, do not assume it completed; Bibi attempts to finish interrupted account deletion when its library next opens.

Log out ends the local session and preserves stored data. Neither logout nor local account deletion removes original Photos items, Apple purchase records, Supabase request records or copies in backups. Account deletion does not itself request a purchase refund.

Depending on the law applicable to you, you may have rights to access, correct, erase or receive personal data, restrict or object to certain processing, withdraw consent, or complain to a data-protection authority. The available rights depend on the processing and jurisdiction. Bibi currently has no dedicated data-export control and cannot remotely retrieve local-only data for you. For information the operator actually holds, contact trvahai996@icloud.com; reasonable identity verification may be needed, and applicable legal response periods apply.

8. Age requirements

Bibi is intended for people aged 18 or older, or a higher minimum age where legally required, consistently with the Terms of Service. It is not directed to children. The current local entry flow does not verify age or collect a birth date.

Anyone below the applicable minimum age should stop using Bibi and delete the local account data. If the operator learns that it holds information received directly from an underage person, it will take appropriate deletion steps subject to applicable law. The operator cannot remotely inspect or erase a child's device.

9. International processing

The current app does not transfer your local library to a Bibi server in another country. Apple, Supabase, external websites, email and backup services may process information in other countries under their own arrangements and notices. This policy does not promise a specific country of storage or unverified contractual safeguards for those providers.
