1. Scope and service availability
This policy covers Bibi's Shorts, Circles, Challenges, Chats and Me features. It explains both device-only records and online processing. Your installed version and available services determine which features you can use. An unavailable service must not be treated as a successful upload, message delivery, purchase or moderation decision.
Online use connects to Bibi's cloud services. Device-only features and an existing Device archive remain separate; an archive is not automatically uploaded. Online features are available only when offered in your installed app.
2. Information and purposes
2.1 Account, profile and choices
One-tap online sign-in creates or restores a guest account through Supabase. We process its account identifier, session information, chosen display name, biography, avatar, agreement version, acceptance time and adult declaration to provide access, maintain your profile and remember your choices. The normal guest flow does not request a password, phone number, government ID or birth date. Declaring that you are 18 or older is not independent age verification.
Guest sign-in does not make your data fully anonymous or unlinked. Content, relationships, messages and transactions can be associated with your service identity. Session credentials and a separate installation identifier are kept in the iOS Keychain. The installation identifier may survive logout or reinstallation; this does not guarantee account recovery.
2.2 Content, selected media and activity
We process the text, titles, captions, comments, replies, circle memberships, challenge entries, likes, saves, follows, blocks and timestamps that you create or select. These records support the requested content, social features, preferences and safety controls. Online actions store the corresponding records in the cloud; device-only actions remain local.
When you choose photos or videos, Bibi can create an app-owned copy, resize images, prepare a video and generate a cover. Online uploads send the selected media and related records to cloud storage. Files can include sound and embedded metadata; we do not promise to remove all metadata. Your original Photos items remain separate. Importing an archived item requires an explicit upload action.
A device-only viewing-history feature keeps up to the 2,000 most recent video records for local ordering. This limit does not apply to every cloud record. We do not use a third-party advertising or analytics SDK to collect this local history.
2.3 Messages, safety and visibility
Online Chats stores conversation membership, message text, sender references, sequence and time information, and available read-position and conversation preferences. Supabase Realtime supports updates to authorized participants. These messages are not end-to-end encrypted: the cloud service processes their contents and authorized administration may access held records for support, security or legal obligations. A local draft or outbox item is not proof of delivery or reading.
Published content and profile information associated with it may be visible to other users according to the feature's access rules. Conversations are restricted to authorized participants. Reports contain the selected content or profile, reason, details and submission time; blocks record the affected relationship and restrict supported interactions. Online reports can be stored for review, but submission is not a promise of staffed monitoring, immediate review or removal everywhere. Do not include unnecessary sensitive information in messages, public posts or reports.
2.4 Purchases and technical information
Where purchasing is available, Apple processes payment through StoreKit. Bibi processes product and transaction identifiers, purchase source, receipt evidence, confirmation/retry status, diamond activity and the relevant account association to deliver items, prevent duplicate credit and reconcile transactions. Cloud native-wallet records and a separate local archive are not interchangeable. Bibi does not receive your full payment-card number, card security code or Apple Account password through StoreKit.
Online connection services receive the app version, persistent installation identifier and an APNs device token when available. Requests also expose ordinary network information such as IP address, request time and service diagnostics. These are used for sign-in, connection/subscription handling, notification routing where available, troubleshooting and security. A token registration does not establish that push delivery is operational.

4. Permissions and choices
The iOS photo picker supplies the items you select rather than unrestricted access to your library. Online upload is a separate action. Optional native camera use supports your own preview and friend-code scanning; the native call preview does not provide a live call or transmit its camera feed. The native app does not currently capture microphone audio for a live calling service. Selected videos may already contain audio.
A connected webpage may request camera or microphone access for its own features. Granting access can let that page receive the corresponding media; native preview behavior does not describe webpage capture. iOS permission controls still apply. You can deny or revoke permissions in iOS Settings and use features that do not depend on them, or close the page to stop using that feature. Camera or microphone permission is not blanket permission for unrelated sharing.
Bibi does not request native access to contacts, precise location or health data. You can change notification permission in iOS Settings. Declining notifications does not require you to stop using unrelated features. Reading an agreement does not accept it or start sign-in. This policy is a notice, not blanket consent to new processing or a substitute for any specific permission required before sharing personal data.
5. Storage, retention and safeguards
Device records and upload copies use the app's storage; core writes are atomic and protected by iOS file protection. Online records use authenticated access and ownership/membership controls; media access links are time-limited where appropriate. These measures do not guarantee absolute security, end-to-end encryption, or protection on a compromised or unlocked shared device.
Ordinary cloud profiles, content, relationships and media remain while the account or related record is retained, until an available deletion action removes them. There is no automatic fixed-day expiry for all account data. Deleting a post, attachment or challenge entry does not necessarily delete its underlying media or separate safety or financial records. Message text may remain in a retained conversation after account deletion with its sender-profile association removed; personal information typed into the text is not automatically removed by unlinking the profile.
Restricted purchase, order, reconciliation and deletion-job evidence can remain after account deletion for duplicate prevention, unresolved transactions, disputes, security and legal obligations. There is no configured automatic deletion deadline for all such evidence. Service logs, backups and support correspondence have no single verified fixed retention period in the current setup; provider controls and applicable obligations also affect removal. We cannot promise immediate erasure of every copy. Contact us about the information held and any retention limitation relevant to your request.
Local records remain until removed with an available control or by deleting the app's data. Offloading can preserve data. Keychain identifiers, cloud records, original Photos items and existing backups may survive app deletion. Backups must be managed with the backup provider; app deletion does not erase them.
6. Access, deletion and withdrawal
You can edit your profile in Me > Settings > Edit profile and use available content, saved-item, relationship, block and report controls. Bibi currently has no dedicated self-service export control. For access, correction, a copy of information held, deletion or other applicable rights, contact trvahai996@icloud.com. Reasonable identity verification may be required; rights and response duties depend on applicable law.
In an online session, Me > Settings > Delete account starts authenticated server deletion after confirmation. It locks business access, attempts to remove account-owned cloud media, revokes sessions and removes the authentication identity and dependent account records. A failed cleanup is not success: use Resume account deletion on the sign-in screen where available, or contact support. Messages, restricted transaction evidence, provider logs/backups and the separate Device archive have the limits described above.
In a device-only session, Delete account clears the local library, app-owned media, local social/chat/safety records, agreement acceptance and local wallet records. It is not a cloud-account deletion request. Online deletion keeps the separate Device archive. Neither option removes original Photos items, Apple's purchase records, copies already held by other recipients or existing backups, and neither requests a refund.
Log out ends the relevant session rather than deleting cloud data. A guest account may become inaccessible after logout, credential loss or device loss; a new guest is a different identity. Request deletion before losing access where possible. The persistent installation identifier is separate from the account and is not removed by logout; account deletion does not guarantee its removal. Stop optional uploads or connected-page use and revoke relevant iOS permissions to stop future optional collection; this does not reverse prior processing or erase retained records. Contact us to withdraw applicable consent or ask about held data.

Bibi is intended for adults aged 18 or older, or a higher minimum where required. It is not directed to children. An adult declaration does not independently verify age. If you believe we hold a child's information, contact us; appropriate deletion is subject to applicable law and the limits described above.
Cloud, connected-service, Apple, email and backup processing can take place outside your country. Available safeguards and rights depend on the service and applicable arrangements; we do not assert an unverified storage location or transfer guarantee. Information may be disclosed where legally required, limited to information actually held and the applicable obligation.
We will update this policy for material changes and provide notice and obtain additional permission where required before new processing. A document update alone does not activate an unavailable service, upload an archive or authorize unrelated uses. For questions, rights requests or privacy concerns, contact trvahai996@icloud.com.
