Bibi does not require a traditional user account with email and password. Instead, we may provide quick login or similar lightweight access methods (for example, guest access, one-tap login methods, or Apple/other platform-based sign-in where applicable).

Depending on the quick login method you choose, we may collect:

You may create a profile and share content within Bibi, including:
