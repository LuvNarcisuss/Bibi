# Bibi implementation plan

**Goal:** Deliver a native anime community app with the complete local Live, Shorts, Voice, Chats and Me feature paths from the supplied skill.

**Architecture:** Retain the atomic repository, stable identifiers, playback lifecycle and permission boundaries. Introduce a Bibi identity/configuration layer, an independently authored animation catalog, reusable illustrated discovery components and a new bitmap asset vocabulary.

**Tech stack:** iOS 16+, Swift 5, UIKit, AVFoundation, PhotosUI, StoreKit 1, WebKit.

**Spec:** User request plus supplied build-teto-social-ios references. Anime content and newly generated illustrations override the dance-only brand and photographic avatar restrictions. No Phrases. Four tabs. English product copy. No build, typecheck, execution, simulator or tests unless subsequently requested.

## Decisions

- Use the empty requested workspace and a new BibiApp directory; no existing user app is overwritten.
- Name Bibi; development bundle identifier app.bibi.anime (not a claim of store registration).
- Pale lavender canvas, deep violet text, pink selection and cyan accents; original anime characters are explicitly fictional.
- Reuse durable interaction semantics; change composition, catalog, theme and source organization rather than claim a complete original rewrite.
- Remove previous owner's payment IDs, contacts and document URLs. Product IDs remain configurable and unavailable until supplied.
- Runtime verification is deliberately deferred under the user's invoked skill. Static evidence only.
